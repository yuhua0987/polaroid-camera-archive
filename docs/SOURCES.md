## Image sources

Bundled media comes from Wikimedia Commons with explicit per-file licenses.
[ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) lists each author, original file page,
license URL and modifications. Saved license metadata is included in
`data/media-manifest.json`; it is evidence from the research date, not a claim
that Commons itself owns the photographs.

Other reference sources include Polaroid's official product pages, Retrospekt,
Brooklyn Film Camera, FilmPhotography.eu, individual camera collectors,
Dan Cuny, PhotoThinking, Christopher J Osborne, Upcycled Classics,
Darabanth / Axioart and Stanley Rowin. Their review text and unlicensed image
copies are not included. Every applicable photo entry links to its exact page.

## Identification and limits

Source titles, visible nameplates and selected visual checks informed matching.
Some pages mislabel images or contain unrelated product/sample images; known
examples were excluded or reassigned after inspection. Refurbished or modified
cameras may differ from factory appearance. Neither years nor all regional
aliases have been independently authenticated.

Keep source attribution when reusing the catalog. Source links do not confer
permission to copy the entire source page or its images.
